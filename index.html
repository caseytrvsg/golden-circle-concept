Terms & conditions

By using this website, you are agreeing to comply with and be bound by the following terms of service. Please review the following terms and conditions in their entirety and make sure you understand them before using this website and our services.

Copyright

The content in its entirety, including text content, graphics and layout, belongs to the owner of the website and is protected by intellectual property rights and copyright law.

Service

When you place an order with us, either from our website or by phoning in your order, you are entering into a binding contract with us, whereby we provide food prepared with ‘reasonable care and skill’ of satisfactory quality in exchange for your custom.

As a general rule, no food is the same — this is especially true for small local businesses. Please do not expect takeaway food from different shops to be the same, and please ask if you are unsure of what our dishes contain.

Delivery

When you order food for delivery, we strive to prepare and deliver your food within the timings stated. Although some deliveries can run slightly late, we stand by our food and hope you find it a delicious treat.

Our peak periods are Friday and Saturday evenings (17:00–20:00), when we are especially busy. Wait times for deliveries can often reach 60 minutes or more from receipt of your order, so please bear with us during these times.

Alternatively, you can place a timed order for a smoother service, or order later in the evening, when our standard timing of up to 45 minutes applies.

Promotions

Our current promotions are only valid for à la carte orders. They are not valid in conjunction with our set dinners, which already have discounts applied.

Ordering a set dinner along with à la carte items to reach a promotion threshold will also not be redeemable.

Refund policy

Generally there is no refund for food. A request for a full refund may be considered at the discretion of the manager.

We reserve the right to ask you to return any leftover food to show to our chefs, so we can understand your concerns about the food we have provided.

A refund will be issued accordingly once the food has been examined and the proportion of the dish that was left has been calculated.

Changes and cancellations

You may change or cancel your order provided that it has not yet been put through to the kitchen. To do this quickly, please phone 01235 510615 to speak to our reception team.

Allergies

Our dishes are prepared in a kitchen that handles nuts, peanuts, sesame, soya, gluten, egg, crustaceans, fish and other allergens, so we cannot guarantee any dish is free from allergens. If you have an allergy or intolerance, please call us before ordering.
